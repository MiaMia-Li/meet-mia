# SnapStory AI

An AI storytelling SaaS I founded and built independently. It generates comics and visual stories from text or image inputs using OpenAI and diffusion models.

I owned the full product from zero: AI pipelines, async generation queues, Stripe monetization, community features, internationalization, and production operations. This project represents my ability to move quickly from idea to production.
