# Career Story

I started as a frontend developer at Meituan, building merchant-facing systems at scale. I then moved to Kuaishou where I led a frontend team building enterprise platforms for one of China's largest short-video companies.

After that, I made a deliberate shift toward AI product engineering. I spent nearly a year as a solo founder building SnapStory AI from scratch — designing pipelines, shipping monetization, running operations — which gave me end-to-end product ownership that most engineers don't get.

Most recently at Atria AI I worked across the full stack on AI creative tools, moderation systems, Chrome extensions, and growth infrastructure.

The through-line across all of it: I'm most energized when engineering and product thinking are both required, and when the work is close to users.
